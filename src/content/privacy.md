# Privacy Policy

**Important Information**

This Privacy Policy describes how Thepalmistrylife ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our services, including our website, mobile applications, and any related services (collectively, the "Services").

**Data Collection**

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, make a purchase, or contact us for support. This may include:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
- Account credentials and preferences
- Payment and billing information
- Communications and feedback
- Usage data and analytics

**Data Sharing**

We may share your information with:

- Service providers who assist us in operating our Services
- Business partners for joint offerings
- Legal authorities when required by law
- Other parties with your consent

**Your Rights**

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

- Right to access your data
- Right to correct inaccurate information
- Right to delete your data
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

**GDPR Compliance**

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

**EEA Definition**

The European Economic Area (EEA) includes all European Union member states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway.

### EEA Users - Additional Rights
If you are located in the EEA, you have additional rights under GDPR:

### California Users - CCPA Rights
California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination

### Virginia Users - VCDPA Rights
Virginia residents have rights under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA):
- Right to access personal data
- Right to correct inaccuracies
- Right to delete personal data
- Right to data portability
- Right to opt-out of targeted advertising
